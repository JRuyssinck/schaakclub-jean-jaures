Do you sometimes play a game of chess at a bar in Ghent, are you a student, or do you mostly
play online on Chess.com or Lichess? We'd love to invite you to one of our club evenings —
just to check out the vibe, or to jump straight in and play.

## Low-key, no strings attached

You **don't need to become a member** to come check it out. Just walk in on a Tuesday evening —
there's always someone around for a game of **blitz or rapid**, casual or a bit more serious,
whatever you're in the mood for.

## A friendly atmosphere

We're mostly about being **friendly**: you can have a drink, chat between moves, and play
alongside chess players of every level. No stiff tournament feel — just enjoying the game.
